My City's first working resident is Jules, the Accountant, assigned to the Town Hall. Click the Town Hall in the 3D scene or 2D map, or use the resident card, to enter its 3D office. The furnished room uses Three.js meshes for walls, windows, parquet flooring, desks, chairs, bookcases, plants and a seated, jointed Jules. Hands and fingers type at the keyboard; head movement and blinking bring the character to life. The computer screen displays the current report's real totals and review state.

Drag to orbit and scroll or pinch to zoom. Room and Jules's desk controls set camera views; arrow keys orbit when the canvas is focused, and plus/minus zoom. Walls cut away when viewed from behind. The exterior city pauses its rendering while the office is open, and the interior releases its graphics resources when closed. Reduced-motion settings stop character animation. If WebGL is unavailable, the room offers a retry and the report remains accessible.

Jules automatically reviews the latest seven calendar days of recorded expenses when My City opens without a current report, and reruns when relevant expense data or the review date changes while My City remains open. Run review also starts a fresh review. Processing continues after closing the room while My City remains mounted; leaving My City cancels unfinished work. There is no server scheduler, LLM request or background execution while the app is closed.

The job computes totals by currency, compares the preceding seven days, finds the largest category, flags empty/Other/Uncategorised categories, and identifies possible duplicates by normalised name, calendar date, currency and amount. Duplicate findings require human review; no expense is edited automatically. Only recorded expenses are included. Invalid and repeated-ID records are skipped. Current income, recurring bills and investment records are outside this review.

Reports carry a date and a fingerprint of relevant inputs. They are saved in the optional accountantReport field of the existing city state, using the city's existing local and Supabase persistence. No new migration or API key is required. Old saves remain compatible. Cloud sync follows the existing city save status. The resident's desk animation is decorative; the progress label reflects actual review stages.
